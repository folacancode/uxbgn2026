# 8-Week Product Design Starter Plan

A self-paced plan for starting product design in Nigeria. Open the site, work through one week at a time, and tick tasks off as you go. Progress saves in your browser on that device.

Site: https://folacancode.github.io/uxbgn2026/

To edit the plan, change the `WEEKS` and `RESOURCES` lists near the bottom of `index.html`.
